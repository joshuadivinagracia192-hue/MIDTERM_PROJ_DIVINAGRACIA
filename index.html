MY AUTOBIOGRAPHY

NAME: JOSHUA S. DIVINAGRACIA

MOTTO: MISTAKES ARE MEANT TO GUIDE YOU NOT DEFINE YOU, SO MAKE MORE MISTAKES TO HAVE MORE GUIDES...


My name is Joshua S. Divinagracia. I was born on December 23, 2008, and I grew up in Matutinao, Badian, Cebu, the hometown of Kawasan Falls. My family means everything to me. They never held me back from chasing my goals. Instead, they became my inspiration to study hard and do better. My mom is gentle and caring, and she understands me better than anyone.


As a child, I loved swimming at the falls so much that I would forget the time. My mom would scold me for coming home late, but I never minded, because I had so much fun. At home I was cheerful and obedient, but at school in elementary I was quiet and introverted. I wasn't very generous with my snacks and only shared them with people I thought deserved it. Because of that, some classmates bullied me, thinking that being small and soft meant I would just cry. They were wrong. I stood up for myself, and the bullying stopped.


My most memorable years were in high school, when I found true friends who liked me for who I am and never took advantage of me. We shared the same humor and the same dream of becoming the richest people in Badian. We always had each other's backs, and in senior high we shared our lunches every day. I was the only boy in the group, and since I grew up surrounded by women, people often saw me as soft, which we found funny. My family and friends taught me an important lesson: to always be wise in choosing the people I trust.


Today, I am a first-year Information Technology student. I chose it because it offers a good salary and is in high demand, and although I still have a lot to learn, I have grown to like it, because I love discovering and trying new things. I also enjoy watching donghua. My greatest strengths are my adaptability and my open-mindedness.


In the future, I dream of building my own business, maybe a poultry and hog farm. I want to become successful so I can live a decent life, free from money worries, and have a simple family of my own.
